Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

HearThai is owned and operated by HearGlobal LLC ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the HearThai app uses and why.

1. No account, no personal details

HearThai does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

2. Your learning progress stays on your device

Your progress, settings and word lists are stored on your device. If you delete the app, this data may be removed unless it is included in your device's own backup.

3. App usage and crash reports

To understand which features are used and to fix problems, the app sends usage events (for example, which screens are opened and which practice modes are used) and crash reports to Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics). These reports include an app instance identifier, device and app version details, and your approximate country. They do not include your name or contact details, and we do not use them to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or billing details. The app receives a confirmation of what you bought so it can unlock Premium.

5. Downloaded lessons

When you choose a higher level, the app downloads its words and audio from our content server. These downloads do not include any personal information.

6. Advertising attribution (iPhone)

If you install HearThai from one of our ads on the App Store, Apple may tell us anonymously that an ad led to an install. This does not identify you.

7. Children

HearThai is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Deleting your data

Because there is no account, deleting the app removes your progress from your device. To ask us to delete usage or crash data linked to your app instance, email contact@hearglobal.org.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.

10. Contact

HearGlobal LLC, contact@hearglobal.org
